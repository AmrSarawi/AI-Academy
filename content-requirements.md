# AI Academy – Content Requirements

---

## 1. Landing Page

### Required Text Sections
- Hero headline
- Hero subheadline
- Academy overview (short paragraph)
- Key benefits (3–5 bullet points)
- Featured programs section title
- Featured trainers section title
- Testimonials section title
- Final call-to-action text

### Images / Videos
- Hero background image or video
- Program thumbnails
- Trainer profile thumbnails
- Partner / client logos
- Optional promotional intro video

### Buttons / Forms
- Explore Programs
- Register Now
- View All Courses
- Contact Us
- Newsletter subscription form (email field + submit button)

### Dynamic vs Static Content
- Static:
  - Hero text
  - Academy overview
  - Key benefits
- Dynamic:
  - Featured courses (latest or selected)
  - Featured trainers
  - Testimonials (if admin-managed)
  - Upcoming events preview
